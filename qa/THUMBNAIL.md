# Thumbnail & title audit

Generated by `node scripts/thumb-audit.mjs`. **Do not hand-edit.**

Corpus: **93 scripts**, 93 with generated `meta.title`.

## 1. Legibility at real feed size

The generator renders 1280×720 (`thumbnail.ts:16-17`); a YouTube feed card is 168×94, so every size is multiplied by **0.131**. Under ~8 px, text is texture rather than words.

| Element | Rendered px | At 168×94 | Legible? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| headline, largest start size | 138 | **18.0** | yes | `thumbnail.ts:139` |
| headline, smallest allowed | 60 | **7.8** | **no** | `thumbnail.ts:118` |
| category pill | 30 | **3.9** | **no** | `thumbnail.ts:78,134` |
| brand line | 29 | **3.7** | **no** | `thumbnail.ts:174` |
| subject label (variant 2) | 26 | **3.3** | **no** | `thumbnail.ts:110` |

So only the headline survives the feed. The category pill, the brand line and variant 2's subject label are all under 4 px — they are decoration at full size and noise at feed size.

## 2. Headline length

The headline is `topic.split(":")[0]` and wraps to at most 3 lines. Guideline is ≤ 4 words.

| Words | p10 | p50 | p90 | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| headline | 2 | **5** | 11 | 12 |

**56 of 93 (60%) exceed 4 words.** A 12-word headline wraps to 3 lines and drops toward the 7.8 px floor.

## 3. Does the thumbnail complement the title, or repeat it?

**37 of 93 (40%)** have a headline whose content words are ≥80% contained in the title. Those cards spend the viewer's only glance restating text that is already printed underneath them.
