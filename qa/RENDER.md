# Rendered-video audit

Generated by `node scripts/render-audit.mjs --all`. Worst-first. **Do not hand-edit.**

Frames sampled at 4 fps and diffed at 64×36 greyscale. "Frozen" = mean per-pixel difference < 0.05 for ≥ 3s — nothing perceptible changed. A "cut" is a gap ≥ 8. Median motion is the typical gap: it separates "holding a still card" from "animating", which the frozen threshold alone cannot.

A/V skew is video length minus audio length, and a positive number is **expected**, not drift: long-form ends with a 5.2 s outro plus a 0.6 s hold and a scene tail, so ~7 s is correct. What would be a real defect is a *negative* skew, or a long-form skew far from ~7 s.

`actual/est` compares the render against `pacingReport().estSeconds`, which counts **speech only** — it excludes inter-beat gaps, scene tails and the intro/outro. So it is a floor, and 1.03-1.17 is the engine's own padding, not an estimate error.
