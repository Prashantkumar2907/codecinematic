# Per-painter motion audit

Generated by `node scripts/motion-check.mjs`. Worst-first. **Do not hand-edit.**

Measured on `/probe`, 60 samples across each scene's own timeline, diffed at 64x36 grey —
the same grid `render-audit` uses, but without the progress bar and captions that repaint over a
frozen scene in a finished video. A flat stretch here means the painter stopped.

**Dead window** = longest run under 0.05 mean per-pixel change, as a FRACTION of the scene.
Seconds are shown too but are SYNTHETIC — /probe assumes 8s per beat (median scene 32s, max 112s)
against real beats of ~4-6s, so only the fraction transfers to a real render. **Front-load** = share of all
motion spent in the first 15% of the scene; a card that arrives and freezes approaches 100%.
**Events** = discrete reveals (spikes > 3x median); an ambient float has none, an authored scene has several.
**Still %** = share of ALL samples below the threshold, however scattered — the metric that catches a scene
motionless in short bursts. **Score** (rubric v2 s6, occupancy): 5 needs dead < 25%, still < 40%, front-load <= 60%, >= 2 events.

**Paint budget**: real-time capture at 30fps allows 33.3ms/frame, judged on p90 (frame 0 pays three.js build cost).
64 of 222 kind/aspect pairs exceed it.
