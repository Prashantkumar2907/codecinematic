> **On the two definition-opener rows.** The loose pattern is the one
> `improvement_plan.md` §1 used to report "first spoken beat is a definition — 30%".
> It reproduces that number, and it is wrong: it matches any `X is Y` sentence, so it
> flags concrete second-person cold-opens ("Your div-button is a trap. A keyboard user
> just hit Tab, and your entire UI just broke.") as definitions. Of its matches, the
> overwhelming majority open `Your…`/`You…`/`This…`. The strict row is the honest
> measurement. **Do not build a gate on the loose pattern** — it punishes the exact
> writing the plan is asking for.
